FAQ: Shields, Damage & Survival
Citation: Publisher FAQ v1.1 · 25 Sep 2026 · pp. 2–4
Official clarifications on Shielded, damage and destroyed Units

SHIELDS AND DAMAGE
- SHIELDED is lost only when assigned Total Damage exceeds Shield value (not when it merely equals it), or when the first model is removed. Damage reduction applied before allocation can prevent the status loss.
- Lost Hit Points from the first model's combined HP-and-Shield pool may be HEALED, but HEAL never restores SHIELDED after the status is lost.
- NON-LETHAL DAMAGE contributes to Total Damage and can remove SHIELDED exactly as ordinary damage does.
- An already-Destroyed Unit cannot return when its last model was removed, unless a Mission or specific rule explicitly permits it.
- Damage reduction applies to a Unit's accumulated Total Damage, including previously assigned damage, not only the newest attack.

DEFENSE MECHANICS
- Evade acts on the final Damage Pool, including hits generated by Surge; Surge skips Armour, not Evade.
- Modifiers cannot make a dice target easier than 2+ or harder than 6+. ANTI-EVADE therefore cannot remove an Evade roll entirely.
- Combat Shield may grant an Evade roll against NON-LETHAL DAMAGE and effects such as Psionic Storm or Plague, per its specific rules.

FAQ: Movement, Cover & Deployment
Citation: Publisher FAQ v1.1 · 25 Sep 2026 · pp. 2–3
Exact geometry and reserve timing clarifications

MOVEMENT AND COHERENCY
- Coherency is checked after movement, not again because of subsequent casualties; the Unit retains its last valid status for objectives.
- Gap Clearance applies to spaces between models as well as gaps in terrain. The High Ground edge counts as Impassable for that check.
- A Move or Run that does not actually change position counts as Hold instead. Directly Towards/Away movement requires the maximum possible distance.
- During Charge/Close Ranks, Coherency Links may pass through Enemy models the Unit is Engaged with; links may cross terrain or gaps that the Leading Model could legally traverse.
- Non-leading models can be set onto High Ground using Coherency through an Access Point; Ground Units cannot move directly between disconnected High Grounds.

DEPLOYMENT
- Deploying from Reserves uses printed Speed: the Unit cannot benefit from passive Creep movement buffs before it is on the battlefield.
- Omega Worm and Pylon bases count as friendly Entry Edges, but do not generate new Zones of Influence. A regular forward-deployment effect is not itself an Entry Edge.
- Deploying via a Pylon ends that Unit's Activation immediately; no further action/Active abilities for that activation.
- Deployment from a marker requires Leading Model base-to-base contact and all models outside enemy Engagement Range. If no legal placement is possible, the deployment fails and the ability is consumed.
- Ordinary special abilities are unavailable while a Unit is in Reserves unless they explicitly function there or resolve its deployment.

COVER
- Direct Cover applies if LoS crosses the terrain piece and the model is Within 1 inch of any part of the same piece, even if another part interrupts the LoS.

FAQ: Shooting, Reactions & Keywords
Citation: Publisher FAQ v1.1 · 25 Sep 2026 · pp. 3–5
FAQs on attack batches, modifiers and timing windows

RANGED ATTACKS
- Declare and resolve each weapon Batch's target completely before selecting the next Batch's target; do not predeclare all targets.
- Attacking a legal target Unit may remove any model visible to an attacking model as a casualty, even if that particular model lies outside weapon Range.
- PINPOINT permits shooting an Engaged enemy, but does NOT allow an Engaged attacker to shoot an outside target.
- LOCKED IN checks whether the TARGET has Stationary Status; it does not require the attacking model to be Stationary.
- For Indirect Fire, if any target model is visible the Unit is considered in LoS and does not get the Evade roll granted to entirely out-of-sight targets; non-visible models may still be casualties.

ABILITIES AND REACTIONS
- An Active ability on a Tactical Card still requires an active Unit present on the battlefield, even if the ability affects something else.
- A Unit may use multiple differently named Active abilities in one Activation if costs are paid. A named Active ability is limited to once per Unit per Round unless explicitly stated otherwise.
- During an Activation, the player is limited to one Reaction. REPEATABLE does not bypass that limit; it allows use in distinct Activations when otherwise legal.
- Reaction triggers can also occur outside Activations. Follow the FAQ's one-Reaction-per-trigger limit; when both players react outside an Activation, resolve the First Player Marker holder first.
- Exhausted Tactical Cards cannot use any of their abilities until refreshed, and excess CP/BM/PE paid by exhausting cards cannot be banked.

KEYWORDS
- PRECISION, SURGE and CRITICAL HIT apply to the combined weapon Batch, not separately to each model. Range, LoS and certain roll modifiers are checked per model.
- When a Close Combat Batch is split among multiple enemies, keyword dice modifiers can be assigned to one target pool or divided across them.
- Effects described as 'while within' are checked at the beginning and end of actions/abilities, and apply as soon as conditions are satisfied.
- PLACE is not a movement action: it does not automatically remove Creep Tumors or Force Fields. Size 1 Raptors cannot cross Force Fields if specifically prohibited.
- Numerical bonuses from different named sources may stack; identical BUFF Speed effects do not stack, even if different sources grant them.

FAQ: Missions, Markers & Scoring
Citation: Publisher FAQ v1.1 · 25 Sep 2026 · pp. 2, 5
Exceptions to normal control and scoring

MISSION MARKERS
- Mission Markers can be controlled while their card is Deactivated; activation state does not remove existing control.
- Artefact Hunt: Flying and BURROWED Units may CLAIM a marker despite normally being unable to Control or Contest one. Claimed markers have their own carrier restrictions.
- Artefact Hunt: Adepts carrying a claimed marker cannot resolve Psionic Transfer to teleport; follow the mission's DROP restrictions.
- Supply Drop: roll for Mission Marker Activation BEFORE the Priority Roll.
- During mission/deployment draft, removing a duplicate card removes that physical copy, not all cards with the same name.

VICTORY POINTS AND MODEL LOSS
- When a MORPH effect reduces Current Supply, the opponent receives VP equal to the Supply lost; morphed Units continue awarding VP for later Supply reductions.
- A Specialist weapon cannot be reassigned to another model after its original Specialist model is removed.
- During close-combat casualty removal involving multiple Engaged enemy Units, preserve Engagement with all of them when an alternative legal casualty remains.

FAQ: Faction-Specific Interactions
Citation: Publisher FAQ v1.1 · 25 Sep 2026 · pp. 3–5
Creep, Pylon, force-field, Terran and special-case rulings

TERRAN AND PROTOSS
- Marauder Concussive Shells: check Range and Line of Sight to the Friendly Unit being Charged, not to the Enemy charger.
- Artanis Phase Prism's PLACE (0) places Artanis in Base-to-Base contact with one model in the selected target Unit.
- Psionic Transfer may PLACE an Adept into Engagement Range, subject to other specific restrictions.
- Roach Tunnelling Claws cannot move through Force Fields, which count as Terrain; a PLACE effect cannot clear a Force Field.

ZERG AND CREEP
- Accelerating Creep checks ON CREEP at movement start; the full move gets the bonus even if the Unit subsequently moves off Creep.
- Queen Creep Speed's flat modifier stacks with Accelerating Creep. Glial Reconstitution and Wild Mutation both grant BUFF Speed, so those BUFF bonuses do not stack.
- Numerical IMPACT modifiers from named sources (for example, Raptor/creep/evolution combinations) can stack.
- Enemy models may block a deployment Faction Indicator; the indicator remains until end of Round and then disappears unused if still blocked.
